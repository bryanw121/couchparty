# Music

## Victory music — CC BY 3.0

`kart-victory.mp3` — **“Victory!”**, composed, performed, mixed and mastered by **Viktor Kraus**.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/victory-1
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
The original MP3 is included unchanged. Playback repeats the track on replay, podium and results screens.
The TV displays the title, artist, source and license while the track plays.

`kart-countdown-preview.wav` and the runtime countdown cues are original procedural audio generated by
`web/kart/game/countdownAudio.ts`; the preview is rendered by `scripts/render-countdown-preview.mjs`.
The start signal uses three identical 880 Hz beeps and a longer 1760 Hz GO tone.
It is an original synthesized recreation of the arcade countdown pattern, not a Nintendo audio sample.

## Original instrumental race arrangement

Driver calls now use recorded spoken clips and human impact/celebration recordings,
replacing the previous oscillator voices. See [recording sources and CC0 terms](human/CREDITS.md).
The source page for the spoken Voiceover Pack does not identify its performers.
The hurt and group-cheer contributors explicitly describe recording their voices.
`kart-driver-voices-preview.wav` uses the live recording playback code: Luigi,
Daisy, Bowser, Yoshi, Peach, Toad, Mario, Donkey Kong; each demonstrates boost,
hit and finish in that order. These are not Nintendo voice recordings.

Current races use the original melodies/arrangements in `web/kart/game/music.ts` with
original piano, marimba, brass-like, string and bass sample synthesis from
`web/kart/game/instruments.ts`, percussion and room/echo processing. These are
procedural instruments, not recorded orchestral performances or Nintendo audio.
`kart-instrumental-preview.wav` is an original dry score/instrument preview rendered
by `scripts/render-instrumental-preview.mjs`; its percussion and room mix differ
slightly from live Web Audio playback.
