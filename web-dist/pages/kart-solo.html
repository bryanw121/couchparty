How to race

Auto acceleration drives for you. Slide the steering pad to turn. Hold DRIFT while turning, then release it for a charged boost. Tap ITEM to use a pickup; hold a shell or banana behind you as a shield, then release to throw. Recover (or R) returns you to the track if you get stuck.
